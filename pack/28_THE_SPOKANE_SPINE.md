# 28 — The Spokane Spine

Campaign through-line. The pack is how this was written; live cards are the authority (pack/00).

The spine is one sentence: **get to Mom in Spokane.** Two hundred and eighty miles. The pass is closed. Everything else — the lot, The Grid, Central, the clipboard — is what she does because that road is not open yet.

---

## 1. The distance

I-001 names the miles. I-011 Mom names the pass still closed. I-012 leaves the lot without a license. II-000 names the empty passenger seat. The miles do not change. The official path to being allowed on that pass does.

## 2. The permit

Ali is **nineteen** with a learner permit. Washington wants a licensed driver in the passenger seat — five years of driving behind them — watching. There is no one at the counter and no one left to ask. Gracie sits where that person would sit. The standard does not go away because the office is empty. **She holds it.**

Do not write her as seventeen once this file exists. The bible follows this file on age.

## 3. Act I — the day the official path closed

Act I is **not** Certification. Do not title it that. Do not treat the lot as a license she earned.

Act I is the day the official path closed: Skill one through four in a dead Fred Meyer lot, the DOL sequence, Mom on the radio, one cat left. She rolls onto a twenty-five without a license in her pocket. The curriculum still applies. The stamp does not.

## 4. Act II — she drives alone

Act II is The Grid. Ali drives. **There is no supervising driver in the seat.** II-000 is the dossier that says so, before the insulin run is named.

Deac is **not** Act II's supervising driver. She may see the Ledger in convoy (II-006, II-016, II-022) as a truck ahead that talks with its arm. That is not meeting him. That is not a licensed adult in her passenger seat. **She meets Deac in Act III.** Act II she drives alone.

## 5. The mock exam, the clipboard, and who administers

The endgame exam is a **convoy standard** — the column will not take a driver who cannot merge, signal, or hold a gap. It **is** the DOL test anyway, because Deac administers it from the state book: real Washington knowledge and skills, the same headings as pack/07 and pack/08, logged on the clipboard.

The clipboard is the PSDP supervised-driving log. In Act II she keeps it because the standard is hers. In Act III it becomes diegetic in his hands: he is the reason the hours get written down. The mock is not a fake quiz with made-up rules. It is the state test, given by a 26-year operator, because the office that would have given it is gone.

Do not write a second, unofficial exam. Do not write Deac into Act II as the person in the seat. Do not call Act I a certification.
