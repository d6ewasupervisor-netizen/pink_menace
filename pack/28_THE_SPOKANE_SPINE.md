# 28 — The Spokane Spine

Campaign through-line. The pack is how this was written; live cards are the authority (pack/00).

The spine is one sentence: **get to Mom in Spokane.** Two hundred and eighty miles. The pass is closed. Everything else — the lot, The Grid, Central, the clipboard — is what she does because that road is not open yet.

---

## 1. The distance

I-001 names the miles. I-011 Mom names the pass still closed. I-012 leaves the lot without a license. II-000 names the empty passenger seat. The miles do not change. The official path to being allowed on that pass does.

Three locks hold that road. They open together, and only in §5:

1. **License standard** — she left the lot without a stamp. The world cannot issue one.
2. **Convoy trust** — a column will not take a driver who cannot merge, signal, or hold a gap.
3. **Road home** — Mom names the pass still closed. Two hundred and eighty miles.

## 2. The permit

Ali is **nineteen** with a learner permit. Washington wants a licensed driver in the passenger seat — five years of driving behind them — watching. There is no one at the counter and no one left to ask. Gracie sits where that person would sit. The standard does not go away because the office is empty. **She holds it.**

Do not write her as seventeen once this file exists. The bible follows this file on age.

## 3. Act I — the day the official path closed

Act I is **not** Certification. Do not title it that. Do not treat the lot as a license she earned.

Act I is the day the official path closed: Skill one through four in a dead Fred Meyer lot, the DOL sequence, Mom on the radio, one cat left. She rolls onto a twenty-five without a license in her pocket. The curriculum still applies. The stamp does not.

## 4. Act II — she drives alone

Act II is The Grid. Ali drives. **There is no supervising driver in the seat.** II-000 is the dossier that says so, before the insulin run is named.

Deac is **not** Act II's supervising driver. She may see the Ledger in convoy (II-006, II-016, II-022) as a truck ahead that talks with its arm. That is not meeting him. That is not a licensed adult in her passenger seat. **She meets Deac in Act III.** Act II she drives alone.

## 5. The exam — Deac runs the real WA DOL test from the book

**This section supersedes earlier pack/28 exam wording.** The exam is not a convoy standard that happens to look like a test. It is not a restored mobile DOL.

**THE EXAM IS DEAC RUNNING THE REAL WA DOL TEST FROM THE BOOK.**

State agency is gone and not coming back in this game. Deac administers the actual Washington knowledge test and the actual skills evaluation from the manual on his clipboard.

### Why

- **Real educational payoff.** WA knowledge plus WA skills. The same headings as pack/07 and pack/08.
- **The license is something the world can no longer issue.** She earns the standard, verified by a person.
- **Completes the clipboard arc.** The clipboard is the PSDP supervised driving log. In Act II she keeps it because the standard is hers. In Act III it is diegetic in his hands. The exam is where those hours get scored.
- **Pays off Deac's silent assessment** across prior acts. He has been watching. This is when he writes the grade.

### Endgame structure

1. **Knowledge test.** Cards from every act, unseen order, no debriefs until the end. Pass/fail against the real Washington knowledge-test threshold. Every card cites `psdp_skill` and `dol_section`. Citation validator unchanged (`scripts/validate-citations.js` — headings must match pack/07 and pack/08 exactly).
2. **Skills evaluation.** Deac in the passenger seat with the clipboard, scoring the state maneuvers she already practiced in Acts I–III. She sees him mark. She does not see what he writes.
3. **Passing unlocks the pass.** The three locks in §1 open together: license standard, convoy trust, road home.

### Writing rule

Deac does not soften. If she fails he says so and she runs it again. He never grades her on anything except the driving.

Do not write a restored DOL office or a mobile DOL. Do not write a convoy certificate that stands in for the test. Do not write Deac into Act II as the person in the seat. Do not call Act I a certification. No exam cards in this file — this is the lock, not the deck.
