# 33 — Lot memory (two mechanisms)

After the DOL lot, the world notices her. Not four systems — two.

## Mechanism one: physical debt

Wrong answer schedules a later card (existing `schedules_callback` / `callback_of`).

- I-003 miss → **II-031 Into the Dash**. Hard stop puts Gracie on the dash. Scene names the cause in the clause: *You never buckled in at the lot…*

## Mechanism two: state stamp

`runs.lot_state` ∈ `called` | `correct` | `slow`, written when **I-006** resolves (`lotKeyFromOption`).

- Option (a) call her loudly → `called`
- Option (b) get low and quiet → `correct`
- Options (c) carrier / (d) wait → `slow`

The value is never shown to the player. It only produces different lines.

Alternate lines on existing cards via `lot_voice` (applied in `applySequenceTone`):

1. **II-001** Act II open (`scene_append`) — II-000 "No One in the Seat" is not seeded; attach here until it is.
2. **II-005** Marisol, first other person in Act II (`scene_append`). `correct` has no line. They do not grade her.
3. **III-001** Deac ride-along (`ride_open`) — one line before his first teaching line.
4. **II-011** first Act II mom radio window (`scene`) — Window A, about a third of the way through.

Act I already stamps tone on **I-008** (`lot_states`) and **I-011** (`lot_voice.scene`).

No new screens. No two-route jobs. No Mya rumor gated on calling.

## Mom radio windows (Act II)

Same register as I-011: she asks about the cats, mentions the pass, never asks how the driving is going.

- **II-011** Window A — Gracie eating, the other one left alone, more snow on the pass, then the lot_state line, then the window closes.
- **II-025** Window B — street names said back, be careful with her whole voice, then the window is gone. No lot_state line.

## Watch
